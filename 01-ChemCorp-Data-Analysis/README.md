# 🧪 ChemCorp Inc. Data Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes business performance data for ChemCorp Inc., a Fortune 500 chemical manufacturer.

The project uses Tableau to transform business data into interactive visualizations and dashboards, helping identify performance trends, compare business segments, and communicate insights for data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

ChemCorp needs to understand how different business sectors are performing in terms of revenue and profitability.

The analysis focuses on identifying performance differences across sectors and understanding which areas contribute most to overall business performance.

## 🔍 Analysis Focus

* Revenue performance
* Profitability and profit margins
* Sector-level performance
* Performance trends
* Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Comparison of business segments
